Limit the Max Speed of your Apollo v4x Scooter

It has happened to us all. There you are, out on your scooter on private land, enjoying your PEV entirely legally, wind in your hair and not a care in the world. And out of the corner of your eye you see an unmistakable figure....Gran. Gran wants a go on your scooter, and whilst Gran is cool, she also has a dodgy hip and osteoporosis - and the last thing you want is for her to hurt herself. So, what you need to do is limit your max speed to 15 mph, which is coincidentally just below the maximum allowed speed for a scooter in the EU. Safe AND Legal - just what Gran needs.

There are 2 different ways to keep gran safe...

1. ESP32 - Flash the code, add power. Board sends 15mph speed limit over Bluetooth until the scooter responds
2. Bluetooth apk - Open the app, it sends 15mph limit over Bluetooth then closes down up acknowledgement
